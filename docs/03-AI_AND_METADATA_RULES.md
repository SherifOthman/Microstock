# 03 - AI Metadata Rules & Prompt Engineering

## 1. Vision AI Architecture (`masterPrompt.ts`)

The Metadata Studio leverages Google Gemini Vision models to inspect downsampled visual previews and extract commercial metadata compliant with top-tier microstock agencies.

### Prompt Principles
1. **Commercial Relevance First:** Prompts instruct the model to think from the perspective of an advertising agency or art buyer searching for conceptual stock assets.
2. **Subject Identification:** Isolates the main commercial subject, setting, mood, artistic medium (e.g., 3D render, studio photography, vector illustration), lighting, and intended conceptual utility (e.g., "copy space", "isolated on white").
3. **Generative AI Transparency:** When an asset is flagged as AI-generated, prompts instruct the model to describe the visual scene naturally without adding repetitive "AI generated" spam across titles and keywords.

## 2. Smart 1-2 Word Keyword Engine (`keywordUtils.ts`)

Microstock search algorithms strictly penalize spammy, run-together, or overly long keywords. The keyword engine enforces strict normalization:

### Strict Formatting Rules
- **Maximum 2 Words per Tag:** Every keyword must be either a single word (`business`) or a 2-word phrase (`team meeting`). Any 3+ word phrase generated by the LLM is automatically broken down or refined into 1-2 word concepts.
- **NEVER Concatenate Run-Together Words:** Keywords must never be merged into single unspaced strings (e.g., `businessmeeting` or `isolatedonwhite`). Words must remain cleanly separated by spaces (`business meeting`, `isolated on white`).
- **No Punctuation or Special Characters:** Strips quotes, hyphens, periods, commas, slashes, and symbols (`#`, `@`, `!`).
- **Blacklist & Deduplication:** Strips generic filler terms that cause platform rejection:
  - `photo`, `image`, `picture`, `stock photo`, `ai generated`, `vector`, `illustration`, `wallpaper`.
- **Target Quantities:** Guarantees between 25 and 49 ranked keywords per asset, perfectly adhering to agency thresholds (Adobe Stock: 49 max, Shutterstock: 50 max, Freepik: 50 max).

## 3. Smart Semantic Truncation & Title Sanitization

Microstock platforms have divergent title length rules (e.g., Adobe Stock truncates at 200 characters, while 123RF descriptions prefer concise descriptions).

### Whole-Word Boundary Truncation
- Titles are never truncated abruptly mid-word (e.g., cutting `portrait of a businesswoman` into `portrait of a busin...`).
- The truncation engine searches backward to the nearest whitespace or punctuation boundary, appending a clean period or omitting trailing ellipses that trigger review rejections.

### Hash & Boilerplate Stripping (`filenameSanitizer.ts`, `renamerSanitizer.ts`)
Camera files and AI generation tools frequently produce filenames containing hex hashes, seed numbers, or tool signatures (e.g., `_9710797b_1`, `v1_upscaled`, `33dc072c`).
- The sanitizer automatically detects and purges hexadecimal hashes, version tokens, and dimensions (`4k`, `1080p`) from being leaked into generated titles or descriptions.
- Ensures all titles read like clean, natural human descriptions written by commercial photographers.
